HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Phase 1 (Hide Slaps): Hide 3 secret slap traps on your opponent's 3×4 tray. The opponent does the same on yours.

🍪 Phase 2 (Eat Chips): Take turns picking a covered chip from your own tray. If safe, you eat it peacefully!

💥 Phase 3 (Slap QTE): If someone hits a hidden slap, the attacker gets a moving Power Meter (x1.0 to x3.0). Hit the center sweet spot for a CRITICAL SLAP!

❤️ Knockout: Each player has 3 hearts. Depleting all 3 triggers a FATAL SLAP with exploding zero-gravity debris!

🪙 Points System: Winning increases your points! When you lose, nothing goes (zero penalty).

🤖 Game Modes: Play VS Computer (Robo-Chip in the park) or create/join a room with a 4-digit passcode to play with friends!
